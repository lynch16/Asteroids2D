Destroy all asteroids and enemy ships to proceed to the next level.  Asteroids will break into smaller asteroids on contact. Enemies may spawn alone or in groups to hunt the player down.

Rogue Asteroids is inspired by the 1979 classic Asteroids.
This game was created as part of the 20 Game Challenge Game Challenge.

Controls
Accelerate in direction ship is facing


Yaw ship to port


Yaw ship to starboard


Fire weapon


Hold to boost acceleration
